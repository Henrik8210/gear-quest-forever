# GearQuest Forever

**GearQuest Forever** (`/gq`) is the WoW Forever fork of GearQuest. It answers: *"What should I upgrade next, and how do I get it?"*

This is a **new repo and a new CurseForge project**. The TBC Anniversary addon stays at [Henrik8210/gear-quest](https://github.com/Henrik8210/gear-quest). Do not copy that project's CurseForge ID or GitHub `CF_API_KEY` secret into this repo.

Click a gear slot on your character panel (right-click), browse from the log, or click the minimap icon to open GearQuest.

## WoW Forever

Forever is the live 1–60 Classic+ client (original continents, before Molten Core). TOC interface is **16001** (CurseForge game version **1.60.1**). After you launch the client, confirm `lastAddonVersion` in:

   `World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Config.wtf`

The only install this repo syncs is the live client:

   `C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`

Lists are the Forever scoring model for all nine classes, levels 1–60, both factions, including paladin and warrior levels 1–9. Do not put a hand-written Alliance list back. Scoring rules: [pipeline/docs/FOREVER-SCORING.md](pipeline/docs/FOREVER-SCORING.md). Paladin has a fourth spec, Shockadin, for spell damage in melee. It is in the spec menu, not a talent tab. Holy, Shockadin, Elemental, and Restoration start with two-hand and one-hand both on. Rogues hunt one-hand axes, swords, maces, fists, and daggers on every spec. Assassination and Subtlety are not dagger-locked. The Weapons menu lists those types under Main Hand and under Off Hand. A one-hand weapon belongs to both hands, so one hand can hide a type while the other still shows it. A main-hand-only weapon stays under Main Hand. An off-hand-only weapon stays under Off Hand. A fury warrior uses that same split. The off hand is a one-hand weapon, not a shield. Protection still hunts a shield. Arms stays two-hand. Before Dual Wield at 20 the fury off hand is empty. Hunters filter the Ranged header by Bow, Gun, and Crossbow. Rank numbers stay the real ones. Protection and Shockadin score weapon speed at -2, so a faster weapon loses less when the other stats are close. Holy and Retribution do not.

## GearQuest score

The hunt hover, the lookup, and the reward on the parchment use the game client's item tooltip. Bags, the character panel, chat, and the quest log already did. GearQuest adds the recommended enchantment, the rank line, and the score after the client's lines. A random green shows the base piece, and one line under it names the jackpot suffix and the chance. On a client in another language, the item name and the stats are that language. GearQuest's own lines use the language chosen in Settings when that language addon is enabled. A sentence that has not been translated stays English. When the client has no tooltip for the item, the hover shows the stored Wowhead Forever tooltip instead, and two red lines sit under it: **Wowhead Forever lists this item.** and **This game client does not have it yet.** The parchment says the same sentence. While the client is still loading the item, the hover says it is retrieving it. The same score trail is on each row of the (i) rank list. The hunt list itself does not show it.

The line is **Rank #N for your level** and a GearQuest index from **0** to **100**. **100** is the best score in that slot at your level. **0** is the worst. On a weapon, rank #1 is that 100. A one-hander worn with an off hand includes the off hand. A two-hander is scored on its own. A shield does not set that 100. The arrow still counts the shield when a two-hander is compared with a one-hander plus an off hand.

When the slot has something equipped, a green up arrow and **+N** means this piece is better by that many index points. A red down arrow and **−N** means it is worse. An empty slot shows the index only. The same item shows no arrow. Rings and trinkets compare to the weaker piece, and only when both slots are filled.

Mousing over a creature, a vendor, a trainer, or a quest giver adds up to three lines. A line appears only when the piece is on this level's hunt list and it beats what you are wearing, or the slot is empty. A downgrade stays off. The line is the rank, the item, the slot, the white score, the green arrow, and the green comparison number, the same +N as the item tooltip. The arrow is never printed without that number. Attackable mobs can also show a second block, **Your top world drops from this mob**, for generic world drops whose level range fits the mob. Named drops stay on that creature's own rows. Those lines only show while you hold Shift, unless **Tooltips → Always show unit gear tips** is on. Without Shift the tooltip ends with one grey line, **Hold Shift to see BiS items from this NPC**, and pressing or releasing Shift updates it. World-drop lines on a hostile mob come from this level's hunt lists, at most ten generic world drops a slot. A ding or a spec change builds that list again. Changing zone does not. **Hide unit gear tips** skips that work until you turn tips back on, and it drops a world-drop list that was already built. The Tooltips window's left example is a sample, **Elder Crag Coyote**, with **Icemail Jerkin**, **Blackforge Cowl**, and **Blackforge Bracers**. It stays visible when real mobs still need Shift, and it is not the gear you are wearing. When the minimum drop chance hides every line, the grey sentence names the Tooltips page and **Minimum drop chance on unit gear**. Named drops, vendors, trainers, and quest givers do not wait. Opposite-faction NPCs get no GearQuest lines, because those drops are not on your hunt list. **Tooltips** on the log footer opens the tooltip settings window: live previews on the left and right, hide tips, always show, drop **%** (when GearQuest knows it), a minimum drop **%** slider, source-type filters, recommended enchantment, GearQuest score, and other-spec ranks. Hunt settings no longer holds those controls. Inside a dungeon the same lines still appear when the creature's name can be read. A hidden name stays quiet and does not error. Lookup stays empty until a result is clicked. A completed quest whose reward you did not take stays on Active in red.

A set piece can stay rank 1 because the set is scored together even when another piece in the slot has a higher score on its own. The tooltip then adds **Rank 1 when you wear multiple pieces of this set.** Enhancement at 26–27: **Armor of the Fang** stays rank 1 for Embrace of the Viper; **Garb of Florid Feathers** can still read **+100**.

If the piece is gear and it is not the ranked row, the tooltip says why: the ranked suffix (**Only Pathfinder Belt of the Falcon is ranked for your level**), another level range, another spec, class, or faction, or **GearQuest is not scoring this item for your class.** Food and other things you cannot wear get no line. Bags, quivers, and ammo are skipped too. A chat link uses the same rank as the hunt list. **Kodohide Legguards** leaves the Enhancement list at 25–27 and is rank 11 at 28.

**General settings → GearQuest score on tooltips** is on by default. Simulation mode shows **Turn off simulation mode to see your GearQuest Score for this item.** The Simulator button says **Turn off simulation**.

**Hunts → Recommended gear enchantment** is off until you turn it on. The tooltip then adds one line just above the rank line. Only the words **Recommended enchantment** are yellow. The rest is the formula and the effect, in white. It uses the class and spec on screen, including while you are simulating. Enchant formulas, armor kits, and scopes are for every character. You do not need the profession. Armor on that line counts for a tank only: Protection warrior, Protection paladin, Bear, and Enhancement Tank. A scope is a bow, a gun, or a crossbow. A wand and a thrown weapon get no scope. **Enchant Cloak - Defense** is +30 armor, **Greater Defense** is +60, and **Superior Defense** is +70.

When you are not simulating, the log header is the **GearQuest Index Score** bar. It averages the index of every gear slot, from 0 to 100, in four sections of 25: green, then blue, then purple, then orange. An empty slot is 0 in that average. The hover lists the same number, and an empty slot says **(empty slot)** after it. The list is highest index first. White text above the bar reads **GearQuest Index Score - Collecting feedback on this**. **General settings → GearQuest score bar** is on by default. Off restores the viewing-upgrades line. The bar updates when you equip or remove a piece. Hovering it does not keep checking. Item tooltips also list the rank the item has at your level for each of your class's other specs, so you can gear two specs at once without tracking hunts for both. A spec that does not rank the item at your level reads **Holy: Not ranked for your level** in grey. An item no spec of your class ranks gets no lines. **Tooltips, on the item column, Ranks for your other specs** is on by default. Off hides the specs you have not selected. The spec you are playing or simulating stays. When that spec has no rank at your level, the line is grey, **Enhancement: Not ranked for your level**, and it stays when the others are hidden. When it does rank the piece, the score line names it, **Enhancement (your selected spec): Rank #N**. A **GearQuest** header sits above that block. Both settings pages are grouped under headings: General has Interface and Tooltips and score, and Hunt settings has Hunt list, Tooltips, and Arrows, map and tracker.

On the active list, a slot whose rank #1 you already have reads **- Rank #1 BiS acquired**. Up to three rank #1 pieces you do not have, the ones that improve what you are wearing the most, read **- Recommended**. A later rank is never the recommendation.

## Where a piece is pinned

A new item stores every real spot in the same pass it is added: the first quest in the chain, a dungeon or raid entrance, a named creature, every vendor, and a published camp in each zone a dropping creature lives in (up to three spawns per zone when they are far apart). The log offers the closest. Updating an item's quest, source, or faction indexes that coordinate in the same pass. A stat or tooltip change leaves the stored spots. Do not invent a coordinate. Heart of Disruption starts at level 30. Alliance pins Emissary Jacques in Hillsbrad Foothills (48.3, 60.1). Horde pins Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill (61.4, 20.8). The level-60 dungeon sets (**Feralheart**, **Beastmaster**, **Heroism**, **The Five Thunders**, **Darkmantle**, **Deathmist**, **Soulforge**, **Virtuous**, **Sorcerer's**, and the Forever copies) are quest rewards at 60. The pin is the start of the chain, **An Earnest Proposition**.

A vendor hunt names the seller at the closest pin. **Dirty Leather Bracers** in Mulgore says **Bought from Varia Hardhide.** Lauren Newcomb is the name when the closest pin is hers in Undercity. The tracker uses that same line, and both update when you change zone. A reputation or honor sentence after the name stays.

A vendor with a required standing gets its own line just before Source. The standing clause is green when this character has reached it and red when they are short. **Requires Friendly with Ratchet. Your standing is Neutral.** **You have not met this faction yet** is only for a faction missing from this character's reputation list.

With the gamepad UI on, the log takes the pad while it is open and GearQuest is the window you are using. One press does one thing. A direction does not also confirm or close. The bumpers do not change Active, Completed, or Removed. The left bumper goes to the previous page, up the side tabs, and the right bumper goes to the next, down the side tabs. From Log the left bumper wraps to Settings, and from Settings the right bumper wraps to Log. The level box and the search box open the on-screen keyboard, and B leaves that box. A crafted hunt stays a profession. The Wowhead Forever plan page names who sells or drops the pattern, and that sentence follows the craft line. Both factions keep the hunt: do not pin only the Barrens stall when Redridge sells it too, or the Alliance list drops the piece and a worn one scores 0. A crafted Protector's, Sentinel's, or Champion's piece keeps both factions too. Those words are also PvP ranks, and that rank gate does not apply to a profession. **Brawler's Leather Hood** is Leatherworking, bought from Pawani or Daniel Stitchsong. **Veteran's Chain Helm** is the blacksmithing pattern those bosses drop. Leather helms whose own page names a boss stay boss drops. A quest that rewards the pattern stays on that sentence (`Reward from the quest "Moonglow Vest".` is Alliance). A pattern that drops from many creatures says the pattern is the drop. A page that has not been read stays the craft skill line, with no pin. **Guise of Battle** is a world drop and has no camp pin. **Scout's Medallion** requires Honored with Warsong Outriders. **Sentinel's Medallion** requires Honored with Silverwing Sentinels. The parchment shows that standing and the standing this character has now.

A worn piece that is not on this character's hunt list is not a score of 0. The score bar scores the worn tooltip when the hunt list has no stored score. A 0 from that read means the client has not loaded the item yet, and the bar fills in when it arrives. Do not return 0 the moment the stored score is missing. The hunt line uses the client item quality when the client has the item, so a green tooltip is a green hunt name. A tooltip stat change is rescored for every class that lists the item before it ships. Hunt settings sets the map pin size from 50% to 150%, and that size stays put when the map zooms. Do not ignore the parent scale. OpenMap makes the icon gigantic that way.

Leveling up, changing zone, logging in, and opening a merchant spread the paper-doll arrows and the hunt list across frames. The arrows you already have stay until the new set is ready. A bag change remembers which hunts you already have, so opening a bag does not walk the whole catalog again. The paper-doll arrows paint one slot per frame after login, a zone change, or a vendor. Ranking up a profession still turns a red skill line green. Opening the log or a filter keeps the current rows until the rank lists are built, then swaps them in once. Hovering a tooltip and switching class in the Simulator are unchanged.

## Guide and the world map

Each tracked hunt has **Enable Guide**. One hunt is guided at a time, stored on that character (`GearQuestForeverCharDB.guideEntryId`). A logout countdown clears it. `/reload` and the AddOn List Reload button keep it. Those buttons call a protected reload, so the addon does not replace `ReloadUI` or `C_UI.Reload`. Tracked hunts stay either way. **Hide guide arrow** on the Hunts page hides the arrow for the whole account (`settings.hideGuideArrow`).

The arrow sits just above the tracker, bottom-left of the arrow on the top-left of the tracker, with a 4px gap. Dragging the arrow moves the tracker. The art is `GearQuest/Art/GQ-GuideArrow3D.png`, a 32-frame horizontal strip, 4096×128, each cell 128. It is drawn at 110×110. Frame 0 points the way you are facing, including west. The frame changes with `SetTexCoord`. The texture is never rotated. Inside 12 yards the line says **Here** and the arrow uses the frame that points back at you.

The guide samples your position once every 5 seconds and turns the arrow from that sample. The closest camp updates on a zone change, when you track the hunt, and when you select it.

On the same continent the line is yards, from the world-position delta. On another continent the arrow aims at the nearest dock this faction can board whose other end is the hunt's continent. The dock is found from the map, so a client in another language still aims at the ship, zeppelin, or skycutter. The line names the dock: **Ship in Menethil Harbor**, **Ship in Auberdine**, **Zeppelin in Durotar**. Those words follow the selected language when it has them. The world-map waypoint stays on the hunt. Arrival on that continent aims at the pin again.

Published docks only. Horde zeppelins: Durotar 50.8, 13.6, Tirisfal Glades 61.0, 59.0, and Grom'gol in Stranglethorn Vale 31.5, 29.6. Both factions: Ratchet 63.6, 38.7 and Booty Bay 26.0, 73.2. Alliance ships: Auberdine 32.7, 43.7 and Menethil Harbor 4.7, 57.0; Menethil 5.0, 63.0 and Theramore 71.0, 56.0.

When a hunt has several spots for this faction, the coordinate line and the guide use the closest. That spot stays until another is at least 20 yards closer, so a camp of spawns does not swap the line on every step.

Tracked hunts pin the world map. The minimap does not. The pins are GearQuest's own frames on the map. They are not registered with the map's pin list, so closing the map with a gamepad does not block jump or talking to an NPC. The icon matches the source: skull for a boss, chest for a container, the Looking For Group eye for trash, that profession's picture for a profession hunt, yellow exclamation for a regular quest, the client's dungeon-quest icon for a dungeon quest, including in front of the parchment title, silver dragon for a rare, orange circle for unsourced, sack for a vendor, and the sunset picture shipped with the addon for a world drop. Pins stay about the same size on the client's continent map and on OpenMap. The client does not have the Far Sight icon, so that path draws nothing. The guided pin has a blue ring. Left-click guides. Right-click opens that hunt in the log. The tooltip lines are **Left-click to Guide** and **Right click to open Hunt**.

The Filter list uses the same icons, after the checkbox and before the name. The Quest reward flyout shows the yellow exclamation on Regular quest and the client's dungeon-quest icon on Dungeon quest. The Profession flyout shows each profession's picture.

The rank list on a slot header opens only while the cursor is on the info mark.

The Track button pulses gold once per account, the same wash as Play Style (`ui.trackSeen`). The pulse runs only while the button is on screen and enabled: a hunt is selected, the log page is open, and the list is not Removed. The first click dismisses it. Hovering Track, in white, says the guide can be enabled. Hovering Show on map, in white, says where the pin is and that the guide can point there.

A generic world drop through level 15 is pinned on a published mob, not the quest camp. Alliance 1–7 is Kobold Vermin in Elwynn Forest (47.4, 35.0). Horde 1–7 is a Mottled Boar in Durotar (41.2, 64.4). Alliance 8–15 is a Harvest Watcher in Westfall (36.4, 50.4). Horde 8–15 is a Plainstrider in the Barrens (47.5, 26.8). **Patchwork Cloak** uses the 1–7 pair. **Calico** uses the 8–15 pair. Quest starts stay where the quest begins. The Durotar quest camp is still 42.0, 68.4. Above 15 both factions share the catalog spot. A named creature stays on that creature.

## Local WoW install

After **every** edit under `GearQuest/` (Lua, `_generated/`, art), sync to the live Forever client. The repo is not what WoW loads until you do. The addon folder is **`GearQuestForever`**.

```powershell
.\scripts\sync-addon.ps1
```

That copies into `C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and nowhere else. It then stages the load-on-demand addons beside the main folder: one `GearQuestForever_<CLASS>` per class, `GearQuestForever_ItemStats`, and `GearQuestForever_Languages`. Staging packs the class hunt rows and shares repeated map spots, and it stops if the packed copy differs from the repo files in any way. The repo files are not changed. See [docs/ADDON-DATA-LAYOUT.md](docs/ADDON-DATA-LAYOUT.md).

The log shows a line under the **GearQuest** title: **simulation mode** (level, spec, class, faction) or **live character** + spec picker hint.

## Languages

Settings → General settings → **Language**. English is the addon language until another one is chosen. A French, German, Spanish, Latin American Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese client follows that client when **GearQuest Forever: Languages** is enabled. That addon is separate and loads only for a language other than English. Disable it to keep GearQuest in English. The first choice needs one client restart. Later choices need a reload.

The window, filters, play style, tutorial, and hunt lines use that language. GearQuest's own item name and zone name do too. The item tooltip that comes from the game stays in the installed client's language. A missing translation stays English. The parchment description, the quest name, and each chain step use that language. A step uses the title the Forever client shows for that quest, so it matches the quest log. An Alarming Request is Une requête alarmante in French and Eine alarmierende Bitte in German. A quest the client has not translated stays English. Quest story paragraphs are in the language addon, one entry per English paragraph. Quest steps and objectives stay. Hunt settings can hide map pins, hide the tracker, and hide hunts ranked worse than what you are wearing.

New player-facing text is an English `GQ.L` key in the main addon. The same key is added to every file in `GearQuest/Locales/` in that same change: `frFR.lua`, `deDE.lua`, `esES.lua`, `ptBR.lua`, `ruRU.lua`, `koKR.lua`, `zhCN.lua`, and `zhTW.lua`, plus the names, db2, rules, sentence, and lore files when the new text is a name, a patterned sentence, or a story paragraph. A translated sentence does not leave an English word in the middle. German keeps the word Hunt. French says traque. The other languages use their own word for a hunt. Slash commands stay English.

When a hunt or an item is added, or its name, description, or story changes, that same pass fills every locale. The name goes into the names and db2 files. A changed sentence goes into the sentence and rules files. A changed story paragraph goes into the lore file.

`scripts/stage-locale-addons.py` builds the language addon from those files. `scripts/sync-addon.ps1` and the CurseForge release workflow both run it. A file that is not on disk yet is read from the local git ref `more-locales`. Do not add a sentence in English and leave the other languages for later.
