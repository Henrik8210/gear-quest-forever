## 3. Full description

### GearQuest Forever

GearQuest Forever tells you the best thing you could be wearing right now, and where it comes from. It is for WoW Forever, levels 1–60, every class and spec, Alliance and Horde.

Left-click the minimap button for the log. Right-click a paper-doll slot for that slot. Right-click the minimap button for the simulator.

On the AddOn List, **GearQuest Forever** uses that same loot sack. Each class row (Druid, Hunter, Mage, Paladin, Priest, Rogue, Shaman, Warlock, Warrior) uses that class's icon. The pictures are only for the list.

---

### Your hunts

*Screenshot 1 — the log, one slot open.*

Each row is a piece for that slot. The grey number is its rank. It matches the (i) list, including #4 and later once you already have an earlier piece.

**- Recommended** marks up to three rank #1 upgrades, the ones that beat what you are wearing by the most. **- Rank #1 BiS acquired** means you already have the best piece in that slot.

A piece you already have leaves the list on that character only. Another character still sees it.

**Hunts per gear slot** is the bar at the lower left of the log, as wide as the hunt list above it. The words sit on the left, the number on the right, and the slider fills the space between. It runs from 3 to 10. 3 is the default. The number shows while you drag, and the list updates when you let go. The choice is saved on the account, including while you are simulating. That number is how many hunts a slot still shows after the filter and the play style. Rank numbers stay the real ones. The character panel, the obtain toast, and **Recommended** stay on rank 1, 2, and 3. A green arrow is on a hunt that beats what you are wearing, including a later rank the slider put on the list. Hovering a hunt fades the gold out toward the right of the row.

### The GearQuest Knight

*Screenshot — the parchment bubble above the portrait, page 1, the Yes button.*

The portrait in the corner introduces the log the first time you open GearQuest on an account. Leave the window up for 5 seconds. He fades in, and he speaks. The title on the bubble is **The GearQuest Knight**. Logging in does not start him. Closing the window during the wait does not count. Once he has appeared, a reload does not play it again. Click the portrait any time to hear him from the first page. Hovering the portrait lights his face. The bubble has no border, and the world shows through the torn edge. The speaker shows one line at volume 1, two at 2, and three at 3.

Page 1 has a **Yes!** button and no arrows. From page 2 the arrows and the counter run **2/12** through **12/12**. **X** dismisses him. The speaker mutes the voice. **−** and **+** are the three volumes, and 2 is the default. The page breaks where he pauses, and the words he points at are black. Page 3 and page 9 print the info icon in the sentence. A gold glow marks what that page is about. The Log, Lookup, Simulator, and Settings handles glow even while you are still on the Log. Page 4 also glows the arrow on the **Weapons** header. He walks the hunt list, the (i) and the slider, Filter and Play style, the parchment, Track and the map, the score bar and the spec picker, Lookup, the Simulator, and Settings. The last page glows the whole window. Making the log larger keeps his tail on the portrait.

With a gamepad, the bubble takes the pad. A selects **Yes!**, the arrows, the volume, mute, and **X**. The bumpers change his page. B closes the bubble first.

### Look something up

*Screenshot — the Lookup tab, with a search result open.*

**Lookup** searches the database for the class you are viewing. Use the Simulator to switch class. A chain reward says how many quests are left. **Thunderbrew's Boot Flask** is Sweet Amber in Westfall, requires 40, and says **5 quests until you get this.**

Quest text uses your character's name. **Wildhunter Cloak** reads as the quest, not the code from the page.

### Where it comes from

*Screenshot 2 — the parchment for one hunt.*

Open a hunt for the source, the quest level, the craft, and the materials. **Track** pins it. **Show on map** opens the spot. **Remove** takes that hunt off the list until you restore it.

A vendor hunt names the person at the pin you are closest to. **Dirty Leather Bracers** in Mulgore is **Varia Hardhide**, not the Undercity cloth merchant. Walk to another town that sells it and the name follows. The tracker uses the same name.

The reward name uses the item's rarity color. A rare such as **Feline Mantle** or **Garb of Florid Feathers** is blue. The strip behind the name is dark enough to read it.

A quest reward says the level it can be picked up, and the level GearQuest recommends you pick it up. The recommendation is four under the item level, and it is never lower than the level the quest actually starts. **Replica Crescent Necklace** recommends 25. A boss drop or dungeon trash hunt says **Recommended dungeon entry at level N.** N is four under the item level, and it stops at 60. **Garb of Florid Feathers** is item level 31, so that line says 27. The line is green once the level you are viewing has reached it, and red before that. A craft says the skill in green when you can make it, and red when you cannot. A burnt mark means the item is new in Forever.

A reputation vendor also says the standing this character has. **Ratchet Wristwraps** reads **Requires Friendly with Ratchet. Your standing is Neutral.** That clause is red while you are short and green once you have reached it. **You have not met this faction yet** appears only when your reputation list has no row for that faction.

If Auctionator is enabled, the hunt hover adds its price lines under the Forever tooltip. The GearQuest score stays underneath. Nothing is added when Auctionator is off.

### On the map

*Screenshot 3 — the world map pin, and the guide arrow.*

The pin is a real place: the quest that starts the chain, a dungeon entrance, a vendor, a named creature, or a camp. GearQuest does not invent one.

Track a hunt and an arrow can point the way. On another continent it points at the boat or zeppelin first. A client in another language still finds that dock. The words on the arrow stay **Ship**, **Zeppelin**, and **Skycutter**.

### Play your way

*Screenshot 4 — the seven play-style cards.*

**No Dungeons** hides dungeon and raid hunts, including a quest that steps inside one. A world drop stays.

**Dungeon Enjoyer** keeps only those dungeon and raid hunts.

**Get it now** waits until you have the reputation, or can make a bind-on-pickup craft.

**Manageable Dungeons** hides a boss drop, and dungeon and raid trash, until you are 4 levels under that item's level. That wait stops at 60.

**Manageable Quests** hides a quest reward until you are 4 levels under its item level. That wait stops at 60.

**Buy it** keeps vendor pieces and gear that is not bind on pickup.

**Focus Zone** keeps a hunt only when the zone you are standing in is that hunt's zone or one of its spots. Quests, vendors, crafts, world drops, and dungeon drops all follow. A hunt with no place stays hidden. Walk into another zone and the list changes. The card sits under Manageable Dungeons.

The two dungeon cards turn each other off. The other five can be on with either. The button reads **Play style*** while one is on.

### Filter

*Screenshot 5 — the filter menu, with a dungeon flyout open.*

Hide a source, a profession, or one dungeon. Boss drop and Dungeon & Raid trash are separate lists. Each flyout has **Select all** and **Deselect all** for the boxes on that list. The button on the main filter still changes every source at once. The rank number does not change because a filter is on.

### Is this an upgrade?

*Screenshot 6 — an item tooltip with the GearQuest score.*

Hover gear in your bags, on the character panel, in chat, or on a hunt. The line is a score for this level: best in the slot is 100, worst is 0. The arrow compares it to what you have equipped.

*Screenshot 7 — the score bar in the log header.*

The bar is your equipped gear, averaged. **General → GearQuest score on tooltips** and **GearQuest score bar** turn the two off separately.

While the simulator is on, the tooltip tells you to turn it off before it will score your item.

### Why that piece won

*Screenshot 8 — the spec weight tooltip.*

Hover the spec name. Those are the weights for this spec. While you level, stamina and armor count for more than a naked +1 of your main stat, and mana counts for less than stamina. Level 60 keeps those same multipliers. The tooltip index is still 0 to 100 for the slot, and the header bar is the average of what you have equipped. An empty slot counts as 0. Filtering weapons does not move the bar.

A random-enchant green is ranked on its best possible roll. The tooltip says how rare that roll is. A slim chance does not hide the hunt.

The premade weights stay locked. Suggest a change for those here: [GearQuest Forever - Class Stat Weights](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jMC89KgzHpPZHYgcjOq6ef7lENhh8OGgypG2rC2m0Ok/edit). A suggestion does not change the addon by itself.

You can make your own. The spec menu ends with **+ custom stat weights**. Name it, pick the premade spec it starts from, and type over any weight. An empty box keeps that spec's weight, including the extra stamina, armor, and mana while you level. A number you type replaces it, including 0. **Save and score items!** reorders that spec's hunts. The portrait spins on the GearQuest window until the list can scroll. Right-click a custom spec to edit it. The weights are saved on the account. A custom list only reorders pieces that spec already hunts.

A Spell power number you type scores Spell Power. The line **Increases damage and healing done by magical spells and effects by up to N** stays on Healing. Leave Spell power empty and that line's damage half still counts as spell power, the same as the premade list. **Stormrider's Leather Pants** says up to 10. Elemental still has it at 25–28, legs, rank 3. Restoration lists it there at rank 13. Holy lists it at 25–27, legs, rank 14.

### Look ahead

*Screenshot 9 — the simulator.*

Right-click the minimap button, or open the Simulator tab. Pick another class, spec, faction, or level. **Turn off simulation** brings the list back to your character.

---

### When you ding

The new list builds in the background, one slot at a time, with a spare frame between slots. The paper-doll arrows rebuild the same way, and the ones already on your character stay until the new set is ready. Changing zone, logging in, or opening a vendor does that arrow pass without clearing them first. Opening the log or a filter keeps the rows you are looking at until the ranks are ready, then swaps the list once.

---

### When it is yours

*Screenshot 10 — the obtain toast.*

Loot the hunt and GearQuest toasts you. The toast names that hunt. A different suffix on the same green is not that hunt.

## FAQ

**The game freezes when I level up.**
A ding builds the new list one slot at a time, with a spare frame between slots, then checks what you just outgrew, then redraws. The paper-doll arrows follow one slot per frame. If it still freezes, comment the version in the window title.

**My FPS drops when I hover items in my bags.**
Update to **v0.5.3-beta** or newer and `/reload`. The window title shows the version. That version stopped the bag hover from loading every other piece in the slot. If it still happens, comment the item name and the version in the title.

**Why does Main Hand show a two-hander?**
Weapons is one header. The arrow before the (i) is the filter. Two-hand and one-hand can both be on, and under each are the weapon types in this level band. Enhancement is two-hand only. Holy, Shockadin, Elemental, and Restoration start with both on, so a staff or another two-hander sits beside the one-hander. The shield stays its own slot. Hunters can dual wield. Shamans cannot. Rogues hunt one-hand axes, swords, maces, fists, and daggers on every spec. Assassination and Subtlety are not dagger lists. Main Hand and Off Hand each list the types in this level band. A one-hand weapon stays while either hand still allows that type. Combat still scores the axe highly. Rank numbers stay the real ones when a type is hidden.

*Screenshot 11 — a hunter's weapon headers, if you have the room.*

**Why is this the best piece?**
Hover the spec. Screenshot 8. The hunt follows those weights. While leveling, a piece with stamina beats a naked +1 of your main stat. Level 60 keeps that same stamina, armor, and mana weighting. The tooltip score is still 0 to 100 inside the slot.

**Can I change the stat weights?**
Not in the addon. Suggest the spec, the stat, and the weight you want on the [stat weight sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jMC89KgzHpPZHYgcjOq6ef7lENhh8OGgypG2rC2m0Ok/edit).

**Holy paladin keeps suggesting cloth.**
Holy levels in mail and plate. Cloth is scored lower on purpose, so a tailoring robe does not beat blacksmithing healing mail of the same band. Hover the spec to see it. If one piece is still wrong, name it, your level, and your faction.

**What is Shockadin?**
A fourth paladin spec in the spec menu, added in v0.5.6-beta. It is not a talent tree. You still start on Retribution. Shockadin hunts spell damage, then holy spell damage, then intellect, then crit. Healing is not scored. Both weapon styles start on, and the off hand stays a shield. Hover the spec to see the weights. Protection and Shockadin also show Weapon speed at -2. Holy and Retribution do not.

**I already have this on another character.**
The hunt stays on this character until this character obtains it. Hammerbone on a druid does not hide it on a paladin. Completed is that character's list.

**The description shows odd codes.**
Update to v0.5.7-beta and /reload. Quest text uses your name. Wildhunter Cloak is the one to check.

**Enhancement is using agility over strength.**
That is the current weight. Agility is priced a little above strength. Suggest the change on the sheet if you want strength first.

**Why does my warlock want healing gear?**
A warlock does not hunt healing. A line that says both healing and damage is scored as damage. Intellect and spirit still show up because they keep you casting. If a pure healing piece is rank 1, name it.

**I am Horde and I see an Alliance reward.**
You only see rewards your faction can get. Defender of Dalaran is Alliance. A Tauren sees the Horde Heart of Disruption rewards, including Striking Staff. If the other faction's piece is still on your list, name it and your race.

**The tooltip says Not ranked for your faction.**
That line is for a piece the faction you are viewing cannot get. If the Simulator is on Alliance, a Horde-only quest reward says that while you preview Alliance. The log header names the faction on screen. Turn simulation off to score the character you are playing.

**The toast named a suffix I did not loot.**
The hunt is the best suffix that green can roll. Shimmering Gloves of Arcane Wrath is not Shimmering Gloves of Healing. The tooltip says how rare the jackpot roll is.

**How do I hide dungeons, or only chase what I can get today?**
Play style. Screenshot 4. **Focus Zone** keeps hunts in the zone you are standing in. **Show on map** is still per hunt.

**How do I look at another level or another class?**
Simulator. Screenshot 9. The item tooltip will not score your gear until you turn simulation off.

**How do I hide the minimap button?**
Open the log, the gear icon, **General**, **Hide minimap icon**. Drag the button around the minimap before you hide it.

**The log is too small.**
**General → Window size.** 100% is the normal window. 150% is half again as tall and wide. Each step is one percent. Let go of the slider and the log, the filter, and the spec menu grow together. The tracker, the guide arrow, and item tooltips stay the normal size.

**The vendor says I have not met the faction.**
Update to **v0.6.0-beta** and `/reload`. Neutral still counts. **Ratchet Wristwraps** says **Your standing is Neutral** in red until you are Friendly. **You have not met this faction yet** is only when that faction is missing from this character's reputation list.

**The hunt tooltip has no auction price.**
Enable Auctionator and `/reload`. The hunt hover adds Auctionator's price lines under the Forever tooltip. The GearQuest score stays underneath. If Auctionator is off, those lines are left out.

**A starter rare still drops a white.**
Update to **v0.6.0-beta**. **Ice-covered Bracers**, **Royal Frostmane Girdle**, **Wendigo Fur Cloak**, **Wispy Cloak**, **Ghostly Bracers**, **Lithe Boots**, **Fingerless Gloves**, and **Ratty Old Belt** are green. **Wispy Cloak** requires level 5. **Ratty Old Belt** drops from Narg the Taskmaster.

*Screenshot 12 — the General settings page.*

**I already have the best piece.**
The slot says **- Rank #1 BiS acquired**. Later ranks stay so you can see what is next. **Recommended** only marks a rank #1 you do not have yet.

**It uses a lot of memory.**
The list is every spec from 1 to 60. A ding, a zone change, and opening a cold log are spread across frames as of v0.5.5-beta. Bag hovers stopped loading every other piece in v0.5.3-beta. If a hitch is still sharp, comment the version in the window title and what you had just clicked.

**GearQuest is not in my addon list.**
The folder must be `Interface/AddOns/GearQuestForever`. This is not the TBC Anniversary GearQuest. The two can be installed together. `/reload`, or restart the client. GearQuest Forever shows the loot sack. Each class row shows that class's icon and says it is only loadable on demand.

**A gamepad popup locks the game when I close the map.**
That was fixed. If it still happens, comment the version in the window title and what you clicked. While the log is open in gamepad UI and GearQuest is the window you are using, the pad drives it. A gold arrow, a smaller chevron inside a larger one, sits beside the focused control and eases toward it. A blue highlight marks the control, and clicking a hunt moves that arrow onto the row. The window gets a soft gold glow that follows the portrait ring. LB sits above the side handles and RB below them. A bar under the window reads A Select, Y Filters, Navigate, and B Close, and an open menu gets A Select, Navigate, and B Close under it. Those marks hide while another window has the pad. Opening a Blizzard window gives that window the pad. A click anywhere on GearQuest takes the pad back. Closing Play Style, the source filter, or the weapon menu clears the highlight, so the blue square does not stay on the hunt list. On the hunt list the arrows and the left stick move through the hunts and skip the filters. Down moves to the next hunt and stops at the last one. Up from the top hunt reaches Active, Completed, and Removed. Left and right move among those tabs. Down from a tab returns to the first hunt. On Settings and the other pages, up and down walk the next control in that column and stop at the ends. Left and right move only when a control is there. One press does one thing. A direction does not also confirm or close. Y moves the arrow onto the filter controls and does not change the list. A second Y returns to the hunts. A on a filter control opens it. The bottom face button confirms, and moving onto a checkbox does not change it. A on a hunt moves the arrow to Track, then the d-pad moves among Track, Show on map, and Remove. The right stick scrolls the text. The left bumper goes to the previous page, up the side tabs, and the right bumper goes to the next, down the side tabs. From Log the left bumper wraps to Settings. The level box and the search box open the on-screen keyboard, and B leaves that box. The right face button closes a menu, then the filters, then the log. Mouse mode does not turn that on. The map still has no GearQuest pin frames while the gamepad UI is on.

**Who is on The GearQuest Community?**
Settings → The GearQuest Community lists Eao, MainWon, Stikmyre, Click, Vaniron, Thrayne, Alteredigor, and Deadlybrew.

**Where is the enchant?**
**Hunts → Recommended gear enchantment** is off until you turn it on. The tooltip then says **Recommended enchantment** in yellow, then the formula and the effect in white, just above the rank line. It works while you are simulating. You do not need to be an enchanter, a leatherworker, or an engineer. A scope is for a bow, a gun, or a crossbow. A wand does not get one. Armor on that line counts for a tank only.

**I do not want one specific hunt.**
Open it and press **Remove**. Restore brings it back.
